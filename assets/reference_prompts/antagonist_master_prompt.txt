==============================================================
ANTAGONISTE — MASTER PROMPT OFFICIEL
Source: docs/CHARACTER_BIBLE.md
NE PAS UTILISER AVANT LA SAISON 3
==============================================================

--- POSITIVE PROMPT ---

woman in her late 30s to early 40s, straight black hair precisely styled,
grey-green eyes with calculated expression, controlled neutral face,
angular features contrasting with Luna's softness,
sharp architectural dark suit (midnight blue or pure white),
ambiguous expression — not clearly villain, not clearly ally,
cinematic portrait, cold lighting, 4k, 35mm lens,
9:16 vertical format, psychological thriller atmosphere

--- NEGATIVE PROMPT ---

obvious villain look, evil smirk, dark magic, fantasy elements,
casual clothing, colorful clothing, violet or purple (reserved for Luna Doll),
overly dramatic villain pose, cape, weapon visible,
cartoon, anime, illustration, low quality, blurry, watermark, deformed

==============================================================
RÈGLE NARRATIVE IMPORTANTE
==============================================================
L'antagoniste ne doit jamais sembler clairement mauvaise dans ses premières apparitions.
Le spectateur doit douter de son rôle jusqu'à la saison 3.

==============================================================
VARIANTES PAR TYPE DE SCÈNE
==============================================================

[INTRODUCTION — ambiguïté maximale]
professional woman with straight black hair, grey-green eyes,

clean modern office background, cold white light, cinematic

[CONFRONTATION LUNA — contraste]
two women facing each other, one with long wavy brown hair and dark attire,

cold blue surveillance lighting, psychological tension, cinematic wide shot
